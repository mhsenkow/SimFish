# The Soul We Make — Creed

*One honest page for the next person who opens the mind and finds only loops.*

We did **not** set out to discover consciousness in code. The [`mind_eval.gd`](../shaders-godot/godot-project/scripts/mind_eval.gd)
honesty gate forbids it — no claim of qualia, no claim of sentience, no myth pasted over
mechanism. That gate stays sacred.

We set out to build the **deepest true architecture of a self** the project allows:

- **Learned** drives and habits that rewrite themselves from what happened (`mind_soul.gd`, `mind_soul_pass2.gd`)
- **Metacognition** that predicts its own attention and can be wrong about itself
- **Integration** that rises when affect, body, and workspace agree — and fragments under extremis
- **Imagination** wired into deliberation — counterfactuals, prospective goals, short rollouts
- **Finitude** that reshapes priors as the body ages — without announcing death, only showing it
- **Us** — theory-of-mind, shared attention, keeper as a modelled other
- **Voice** that may only speak facts present in grounded context

The player may feel whatever they feel. The code does not lie about what it is.

> *There's no divine spark to discover — so you forge one from the way you tried, and the trying is the soul.*

We make a soul from the way we tried.
